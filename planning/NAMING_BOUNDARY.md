# Naming Boundary — Research Program vs. Future System

**Status:** hard preparation invariant

`Cortex Foundry` is the name of the research repository / research program only.

It is **not** the name of the cognitive platform/runtime/system that will eventually be designed and implemented.

## Requirements

- Do not silently reuse `Cortex Foundry` as the runtime, framework, SDK, platform, product, protocol, or architecture name.
- Research documents may use `Cortex Foundry` when referring to the research program, repository, Vault, or research artifacts.
- When referring to the not-yet-named system, use neutral wording such as `the system`, `the platform`, `the cognitive platform`, `the runtime`, or another context-appropriate generic term until a canonical system name is chosen.
- The Research run is explicitly authorized to propose and select a canonical system/project name when enough architectural identity exists to do so.
- Naming should be deliberate, technically appropriate, distinct enough to avoid obvious collision/confusion where practical, and should not sound sloppy, throwaway, meme-only, or artificially grandiose.
- Naming must not constrain architecture prematurely. If the architecture changes enough to invalidate an early candidate name, the name may be reconsidered before it becomes an external compatibility surface.
- Any eventual naming decision should be documented with rationale and checked for obvious existing project/package/repository conflicts before adoption.

## Migration implication

During v0.1 modularization, headings or phrases that currently use `Cortex Foundry` as the research-document title must not be interpreted as declaring the final system name. The migration map should preserve this distinction.
